8020Ride

Terms

8020Ride shows a flat fare for a trip in Porterville and the valley. The number locks when you confirm it on this device. Demand does not change it. If the road router is unavailable, the distance is a straight-line estimate and the page says so.

This release is not a transportation service. It does not dispatch a driver, carry a passenger, charge a card, or pay anyone. The figure under Drive is a record on this device, not money owed. 8020Ride is not a licensed carrier and does not provide the insurance a California ride requires.

The intended split, when a ride is real, is 80 percent to the driver and 20 percent to 8020Ride, taken from the driver's share. That split is not in effect, because no ride is being sold.

Quotes are limited to Porterville, Visalia, Tulare, Lindsay, Exeter, and the foothills. The site is provided as is, without a promise that a route, a price, or the map is fit for a trip you actually take.
