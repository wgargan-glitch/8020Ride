Privacy

8020Ride does not create an account. It does not ask for your name, phone number, or a card.

Trips, saved Home and Work, and the on-device record stay in this browser's local storage. Clearing the site deletes them. They are not sent to an 8020Ride server, because there isn't one.

The map is drawn by OpenFreeMap from OpenStreetMap data. Tapping the map or searching an address sends that place to Photon so it can be named. A route sends the two ends to the public OSRM demo router so the distance can be measured. If that router does not answer, the page estimates a straight line and says so. Those services have their own privacy policies. 8020Ride does not sell this information and does not add a tracker.

Do not use this site to request a ride you need. No car is dispatched.
